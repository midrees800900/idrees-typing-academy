Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how Idrees Typing Academy may handle information when you visit or use the website. The academy provides free typing lessons, practice activities, tests and learning features.

Information and website features

Information you enter into a typing exercise is used to provide the exercise and calculate the result shown to you. Some features may store progress or preferences in your browser or, where an account feature is enabled, associate information with your account. Do not enter passwords or other sensitive personal information into typing passages or feedback fields.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences, measurement and, if advertising is enabled, advertising. Your browser settings can help you manage or delete cookies, although disabling some cookies may affect website features.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If this website displays advertisements from Google or other advertising partners, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure and limit advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable settings. Google may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide advertising services. Where required, the website will provide appropriate consent and advertising controls. For more information about how Google uses data, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The website may rely on hosting, authentication, database, analytics or advertising providers to operate its features. Those providers process information according to their own privacy terms and settings. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

Children and young learners

The academy is intended to be a general-audience educational resource. Advertising settings and any required consent mechanisms should be configured to comply with applicable laws and platform policies, including protections for children and younger users. Please contact us if you believe information has been submitted in error.

Data security and retention

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information used by the website. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide features, maintain security, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Your choices and contact

You can manage cookies through your browser and contact us with privacy questions or requests. Email midrees800900@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
