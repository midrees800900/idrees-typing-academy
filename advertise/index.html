EDUCATION PARTNERSHIPS

Partner with Idrees Typing Academy

Idrees Typing Academy is a free learning platform for people building keyboard skills. We are open to hearing from organisations whose work is relevant to education, digital literacy and learning.

Possible sponsorships

  • A clearly labelled sponsor message on an appropriate information page.
  • A relevant educational resource or learning initiative featured for visitors.
  • A limited campaign supporting free access to typing practice.

Our standards

  • Learning activities and typing tests remain free to access.
  • Sponsor content must be clearly identified and must not imitate academy instructions or controls.
  • We review relevance, accuracy, privacy and suitability before accepting a sponsor.
  • We do not promise visitor numbers, clicks, sales or results.

This page is an invitation to discuss possible partnerships, not a statement that paid advertising is currently active. Any sponsorship, contract and payment arrangements must be reviewed and managed by the responsible adult for the website.

Enquiries

For an initial enquiry, email midrees800900@gmail.com with the organisation name, website and a short description of the proposed educational partnership. Do not send payment details by email.
