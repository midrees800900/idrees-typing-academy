Free Online Typing Speed Test

Measure your typing speed and accuracy with a free online typing test. Choose English, Urdu, Spanish or French and complete a one, three or five-minute assessment.

How it works

A typing speed test measures how many words you can type in a minute. WPM means words per minute; accuracy shows how closely your typed text matches the passage. A useful practice routine is to take a short test, review mistakes, and repeat the exercise later. The academy offers timed tests and stores completed results in your progress dashboard when you sign in.
