HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Is Idrees Typing Academy free to use?

Yes. The typing lessons, practice games and typing assessments are free to use.

Which languages can I practise?

You can select English, Urdu, Spanish or French when preparing a typing assessment.

How long can my typing test be?

You can choose a one-minute, three-minute or five-minute test.

How do I receive a typing certificate?

Complete a test while signed in to receive a permanent, publicly verifiable certificate. Guests can print a local certificate. There is no minimum speed or accuracy score.

Can someone verify my certificate?

Yes. Each certificate has a unique ID and a public verification link. Visit the certificate verification page to learn how verification works.
