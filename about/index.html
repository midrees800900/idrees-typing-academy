About Idrees Typing Academy

Idrees Typing Academy is a free online learning platform designed to help people build practical keyboard skills through guided typing lessons, timed typing assessments and short practice activities.

What learners can do

  • Practise touch typing, including the home row, upper row, lower row, common words, capitals, numbers and punctuation.
  • Take one-minute, three-minute and five-minute typing assessments.
  • Practise passages in English, Urdu, Spanish and French.
  • Review typing speed and accuracy and work towards personal learning goals.
  • Celebrate completed assessments with academy certificates.

Our approach

We encourage learners to focus on accuracy, build confidence gradually and practise at a comfortable pace. The academy's core lessons, practice activities and typing tests are intended to remain free to use.
