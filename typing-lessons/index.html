Free Online Typing Lessons for Beginners

Learn touch typing step by step with beginner-friendly lessons for the home row, top row, bottom row, common words, capital letters, numbers and punctuation. Practise each lesson at your own pace, focus on accuracy before speed, and build confidence with regular sessions.

How it works

Typing lessons help you learn where each finger belongs on the keyboard. Start with the home row, keep your eyes on the screen, and practise short patterns until the movement feels familiar. Move to the top and bottom rows only when you are comfortable. The academy includes six guided exercises that you can open directly from the Lessons section on the homepage.
