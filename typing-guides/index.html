A simple weekly learning loop

  1. Choose one target. Examples: fewer missed letters, steadier home-row movement, or more reliable punctuation.
  2. Practise that target. Use a short exercise instead of taking test after test.
  3. Measure consistently. Use the same language, duration and similar difficulty when comparing results.
  4. Review the errors. Notice which keys or combinations caused trouble and make the next session specific.
Academy principle: A higher speed number is not automatically better if accuracy drops sharply. Aim for controlled, repeatable typing and let speed develop from reliable technique.

Put a guide into action

After reading, open a guided lesson to practise individual keys, or take a one-, three- or five-minute typing test to establish a baseline. Your score is a snapshot, not a judgement of your ability.
